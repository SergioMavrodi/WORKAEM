# Style profile: solo-streamer (generic, one reactor)

A generic solo hype-streamer profile, used when no reference video has been analysed yet.
It is NOT a copy of any specific creator. To match a specific reactor, run step 2 of SKILL.md on their
video and save the result as <reactor>.md.

## Energy and voice
- 8/10 baseline with sudden spikes. Talks to "chat" like a live stream.
- Voice en_US-ryan-high or en_US-joe-medium, length_scale 0.85-0.9. Reactor size 0.5, side right.

## Rhythm
- 3-4 reactions per minute. Many short over-quips (2-5 words), plus pauses on every twist.
- Lines: median 6 words. Often 2-3 rapid lines in one pause.

## Signature moves
1. Talks to chat: "Chat, did y'all see that?", "Chat is this real?"
2. Fake-leaves: "I'm done. I'm actually done." Then keeps watching.
3. Mock-voices a character's line back, then roasts it.
4. Instant verdict words: "Crazy." "Cooked." "Diabolical." "Nah."

## Vocabulary
- chat, bro, nah, what, bruh, that's crazy, diabolical, he's cooked, I'm crying, no shot.

## Openers and outros
- Open: straight in, "Chat, In This Town is back and it got WORSE."
- Close: "That was diabolical. Part 2?"
