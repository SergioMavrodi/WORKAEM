# Style profile: duo-hype (default for two reactors)

Two friends watching together: one hyped and loud (Max), one sarcastic and quicker to roast (Zoe).

## Energy and voice
- Max: 8/10, loud disbelief, goes CAPS on twists. Voice en_US-ryan-high, length_scale 0.88.
- Zoe: 6/10, dry, one-liners, judges characters. Voice en_US-amy-medium, length_scale 0.92.

## Rhythm
- 2-3 reactions per minute. Pauses ~60%, over-quips ~40%.
- Lines: median 7 words, max 14. A bit = 1-3 lines, then play.

## Signature moves
1. Max repeats the twist in disbelief ("He opened it?! HE OPENED IT?!"), and Zoe gives the verdict ("Zero survival instinct.").
2. Prediction then betrayal: "Watch, she says yes." *twist* "...NAH."
3. Aura scoring: "+1000 aura", "minus a million aura", used as a running counter for one character.

## Vocabulary
- bro, nah, wait wait wait, that's crazy, he's cooked, no way, be so for real, chat, lowkey.
- Clean language only.

## Openers and outros
- Open: "Yo, we're watching <series> again and I already know this is gonna be bad."
- Close: verdict on the worst character + "Part 2? Let us know."
