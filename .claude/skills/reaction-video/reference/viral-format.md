# The viral reaction format (story compilations: "In This Town", Roblox stories, TikTok skits)

## Packaging (decides whether anyone clicks)
- **Title**: `<SERIES> Roblox Compilation But Things Get CRAZY... | REACTION`,
  `<SERIES> Roblox TikToks Got WORSE`, `I Watched <SERIES> And I Regret It...`. Use the series name first
  (people search for it), an escalation word (CRAZY / WORSE / UNHINGED / TOO FAR), and an ellipsis or `?!`.
- **Thumbnail**: one frame of the best twist, oversaturated. A reactor face with a huge shocked
  expression takes 30-40% of the frame on one side. 2-4 words of giant text, with one word red
  (`Everyone Gets *Cracked*`) or a "SHE LIKES HIM?!" style question. Add a red arrow or circle on the detail.
- The thumbnail text and the title say different things. They complement each other, they don't repeat.

## Structure
1. **Cold open hook (0-4 s)**: the most insane moment from later in the video plus a title card, then a whoosh into the start.
2. **Fast intro (≤ 5 s)**: one line, like "Yo, today we're watching In This Town and I already know it's gonna be bad." No long greetings.
3. **The compilation**, story by story. Each story follows a rhythm:
   - setup plays mostly untouched (an `over` quip at most),
   - **pause on the twist**, then a zoom, and 1-3 lines (the reaction, a callback, a punchline),
   - optional second pause on the aftermath ("and he just WALKED AWAY?").
4. **Running gags**: pick 2-3 recurring bits (a character you "hate", a rating system like
   "aura points", "-1000 aura", "that's crazy work", a counter of how many times someone gets slapped). Call them back.
5. **Outro (≤ 6 s)**: a final verdict + "part 2?" bait. Pause on the last frame.

## Density and pacing
- React every **15-40 s**, and never let 60 s pass silent.
- Pauses take 20-35% of the output time. A 10-min compilation becomes 13-15 min.
- Lines: 3-14 words. Punchline last. One idea per line.
- Two-reactor banter: A reacts, B tops it or disagrees. Keep each exchange to 1-3 lines, then play.
- Cut: slow intros of the original, repeated subscribe outros, dead air > 2 s.

## Kinds of reactions (mix them)
- **Disbelief**: "Wait wait wait... rewind. He did WHAT?"
- **Roast the character**: "This man has the survival instincts of a goldfish."
- **Predict, then be wrong**: "Watch, she's gonna say yes." Then the twist. "NAH."
- **Moral judge**: "Bro, that's a crime. That's literally a crime."
- **Relatable**: "Every single school has that one kid."
- **Rating / aura**: "+500 aura." / "Minus a thousand aura, instantly."
- **Fourth wall**: "Why does every story in this town end in a hospital?"

## Don'ts
- Don't talk over the original's key dialogue or the punchline itself. React *after* it.
- Don't explain the joke. Don't narrate what's on screen ("he is walking to the door").
- Don't overuse shock + boom (≤ 1 per 2 minutes).
- Don't reupload without real commentary, and always credit the original channel.
